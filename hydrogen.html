Particle Physics Simulation Instructions

  • Turn On/Off Accelerator: Controls the particle accelerator, which generates pions.
  • Turn On/Off Solenoid: Activates/deactivates the magnetic field of the solenoid, affecting muon movement.
  • Bring Muon to Solenoid: Moves the most recently created muon to the center of the solenoid.
  • Observe: Watch how particles interact under different conditions. The electron orbits the Hydrogen nucleus. Pions, when created, decay into muons. The solenoid's magnetic field affects the muon. If a muon gets close enough to the electron, it can be captured, ejecting the original electron. This new muon then orbits the nucleus until it decays.
  • Camera Controls: Use your mouse to drag and rotate the scene.
  • Particle Information: Type a particle name (e.g., Muon, Proton, Electron, Pion, Neutron) and click "Explain Particle" to get information using the Gemini API.
  • Particle Interaction: Enter two particle names and click "Predict Interaction" to get an explanation of how they might interact using the Gemini API.
  • Concept Check / Quiz: Enter a physics concept (e.g., "Quarks", "Beta Decay") and get a question and answer generated by the Gemini API.

Experiment with the controls to see how they affect the simulation!
